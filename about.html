About

Alex Buaiscia

Education, storytelling, and software
githublinkedintwitter

I’m interested in how people learn and in the stories, games, and tools that help ideas become easier to explore. My work brings together a background in language education with software development.

My path into development was not a straight one. Born in Italy, I later lived in Bogotá, Colombia, and Brno, Czech Republic. After studying languages and the humanities, I worked in language education, including teaching and curriculum development. Back in Europe, I worked in application support and taught myself development while building tools for personal and professional use.

Teaching and curriculum development still shape how I work: I think about the learner’s questions, the path through an idea, and the connections that make it memorable. I’m especially curious about history, natural sciences, astronomy, literature, and systems thinking.

Today I continue to work in software engineering while developing educational projects. The Map of Discoveries is a story-based learning app for children, beginning with mathematics and geometry. From Fire to Stars is a printable gamebook prototype about the development of civilizations. PolyAgora is a space for sharing knowledge across disciplines.

I write about what I’m building and learning, including software development and the questions behind these projects. You can find the articles in Writing.
