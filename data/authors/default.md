I’m a software engineer working mainly with JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Node.js, and full-stack web development. I enjoy building useful products and explaining technical ideas clearly.

My path into development was not a straight one. Born in Italy, I later lived in Bogotá, Colombia, and Brno, Czech Republic. After studying languages and the humanities, I worked in language education, including teaching and curriculum development. Back in Europe, I worked in application support and taught myself development while building tools for personal and professional use.

That background still informs my work. I’m interested in how people learn, how ideas move between fields, and how technology can help make complex subjects more approachable. My interests include history, natural sciences, astronomy, literature, meditation, and systems thinking.

Today I continue to work in software engineering while exploring projects that connect knowledge and education. [PolyAgora](/projects/polyagora) is an evolving space for interdisciplinary knowledge sharing. [From Fire to Stars](/projects/from-fire-to-stars) is a printable educational gamebook prototype about the development of civilizations.

I write about software development and, increasingly, the questions and design decisions behind those projects. You can find the articles in [Writing](/blog).
